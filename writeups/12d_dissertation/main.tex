\section*{Abstract}

Type IIB string theory possesses a conjectured non-perturbative \SLZ{} S-duality whose ten-dimensional (10d) origin is not manifest. This thesis examines the proposal that the duality, and the theory itself, descend from a 12d structure, following four lines of investigation:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item We establish a 12d geometric interpretation of the axio-dilaton sector. The D7 brane and D(-1) instanton backgrounds arise as a Kaluza-Klein monopole and pp-wave of 12d gravity reduced on a torus. The \SLZ{} generators act geometrically as large diffeomorphisms in 12d, and reduction of the 12d covariantly-constant spinor condition reproduces the type IIB Killing spinor equations.
    \item We propose a covariant 12d unification of the type IIB form-field sector. The \SLR{}-doublet 3-forms are assembled into a 12d 4-form, and the 10d self-duality condition on the 5-form field strength is realised as a 12d Hodge duality relation.
    \item We review the known link between the worldvolume and bulk dualities on the D3 brane.
    Its worldvolume electromagnetic duality leaves the brane action invariant only when the bulk fields undergo a simultaneous \SLZ{} transformation, identifying the worldvolume S-duality with the type IIB \SLZ{}.
    \item We examine the higher-derivative corrections to the effective action.
    At four points the 8-derivative couplings admit a 12d-covariant form. At five points, a U(1)-preserving axio-dilaton coupling is shown to admit no 12d-covariant expression, constituting an obstruction to the interpretation.
\end{enumerate}
Taken together, the 12d picture remains an effective repackaging of the 10d theory, and we discuss its limits across the brane, form-field, and higher-derivative sectors.

\section*{Declaration of Contributions}

This thesis examines the proposal that the type IIB string theory has a 12d origin.
The greater part reviews existing literature, attributed by citation throughout: the $10+2$d supergravity and superalgebras, the brane interpretations of the axio-dilaton, and the D3-brane worldvolume analysis are all due to others.

\noindent Two results are original.
The first, in Section~\ref{sec:covariant-unification}, is a covariant 12d repackaging of the type IIB form-field sector, in which the 10d self-duality of $F_5$ is realised as a 12d Hodge duality.
The second, in Section~\ref{sec:effective-actions}, is a systematic argument that a $U(1)$-preserving five-point contact term admits no 12d covariant form.
The organization of the algebraic, brane, form-field, and effective-action evidence into a single self-contained account, and the line of argument running through the thesis, are also my own.
