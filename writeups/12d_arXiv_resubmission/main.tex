\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Type IIB string theory possesses a conjectured non-perturbative \SLZ{} S-duality that is not realised geometrically in ten dimensions (10d).
We examine the proposal that the duality, and the theory itself, descend from a 12d structure.
We first establish a 12d geometric interpretation of the axio-dilaton sector, in which the D7 brane and D(-1) instanton backgrounds arise as a Kaluza-Klein monopole and pp-wave of 12d gravity reduced on a torus, the \SLZ{} generators act geometrically as large diffeomorphisms in 12d, and reduction of the 12d covariantly-constant spinor condition reproduces the type IIB Killing spinor equations.
We then propose a covariant 12d unification of the type IIB form-field sector, assembling the \SLR{}-doublet 3-forms into a 12d 4-form and realising the 10d self-duality condition on the 5-form field strength as a 12d Hodge duality relation.
We review the known link between the worldvolume and bulk dualities on the D3 brane, whose worldvolume electromagnetic duality leaves the brane action invariant only when the bulk fields undergo a simultaneous \SLZ{} transformation, identifying the worldvolume S-duality with the type IIB \SLZ{}.
Finally we examine the higher-derivative corrections to the effective action: at four points the 8-derivative couplings admit a 12d-covariant form, while at five points a U(1)-preserving axio-dilaton coupling is shown to admit no 12d-covariant expression, constituting an obstruction to the interpretation.
Taken together, the 12d picture remains an effective repackaging of the 10d theory, and we discuss its limits across the brane, form-field, and higher-derivative sectors.
\end{abstract}
